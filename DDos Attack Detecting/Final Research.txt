Slide 2: The Problem Statement (Why This Research Matters)
Title: The Problem: Over-Optimism and Literature Inconsistencies
Key Points:
The Reproducibility Crisis: Many published cybersecurity papers report near-100% accuracy, but these models often fail when deployed on real networks.
The "Data Leakage" Trap: Splitting data randomly mixes packets from the same connection into training and testing, allowing models to cheat by memorizing connections.
Unverified Architectural Claims: Ahmad et al. claimed that adding Apache Kafka and Spark Streaming increases detection accuracy by 3% to 7%.
The Goal: Run an independent, honest audit using real Big Data to find the truth.
🗣️ Speaker Note: "Many intrusion detection papers claim near-100% accuracy because of hidden data leakage. Furthermore, the paper claimed that Kafka magically boosts model accuracy. We wanted to see what happens when you test models fairly on a completely different day."
